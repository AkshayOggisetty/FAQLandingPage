Before You Post

Group Protocol

Follow these steps before sending your query in the WhatsApp group.

01

Check the FAQ First

Please check the FAQ section on samagama.in before posting your question. Your query may already have been answered there. We also provide a Yaksha MiniBot that can answer questions based on the FAQ, so please try that as well.

02

Mention Your Email

Please mention the email address you used while registering on Samagama. This helps us look into your case more quickly.

03

Provide Details & Screenshots

Kindly provide a screenshot and as many relevant details as possible so that we can better understand and resolve your issue. Instead of sending multiple short messages, please send one detailed message.

04

Use Threaded Replies

If you are already in discussion with us regarding an issue, please use threaded replies instead of starting fresh messages.

You can leave the group once your query is cleared.
